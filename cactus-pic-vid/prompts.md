# Cactus 领读绘本视频 · 分镜脚本

## 总览
- 8 张静态绘本图 → 4 个 Clip（连续运镜写法）→ 总时长 35s
- 风格：扁平卡通 + 粗黑描边 + 彩虹字母 + 儿童教育插画
- 收势：定格式（镜头锁住 + 画面静止 + 不渐隐不淡出）

## Clip 1 · 8s · 开场亮相 [图1+图2]

**首帧**: 1.jpg（彩色 CACTUS 标题 + 黄色沙丘 + 两侧小仙人掌+太阳）
**尾帧**: 2.jpg（黄色沙地上中央双臂仙人掌）

**Prompt**:
```
Camera starts on the colorful "CACTUS" title card with yellow sand dunes below and small cacti on both sides. 
Warm sun glow blooms in from the edges, the title letters pulse gently once. 
Camera slowly pushes forward, scene gradually transitions: the title slides upward and out of frame, 
yellow sand dunes and side cacti blur with a warm golden glow, 
a tall central green cactus with both arms raised emerges from the glow. 
Camera continues to push in to a medium close-up of this central cactus. 
Final frame: camera locks completely, image becomes still, no fade, no dissolve, holds to the last frame. 
Children's picture book illustration style, flat cartoon with thick black outlines, vibrant rainbow colors, warm and lively atmosphere.
```

## Clip 4 · 10s · 高耸+坚强 [图7+图8]

**首帧**: 7.jpg（蓝天+沙地+高大仙人掌顶开粉花）
**尾帧**: 8.jpg（暖橙日落沙丘+6株仙人掌群像）

**Prompt**:
```
Camera starts with a low-angle shot of a tall cactus on the desert with a pink flower on top. 
Camera slowly pushes upward to emphasize the cactus's height and grandeur. 
Camera slowly pulls back, a warm golden glow transitions to a sunset scene. 
Scene transitions: warm orange sunset sky reveals a panoramic view of six varied cartoon cacti standing together on warm orange desert dunes, all in a row, exuding strength and resilience. 
Camera continues to slowly pull back to a wider panoramic view. 
Final frame: camera locks completely, the group of cacti stands still in their powerful pose, image becomes still, no fade, no dissolve, holds to the last frame for a lingering emotional resonance. 
Children's picture book illustration style, flat cartoon with thick black outlines, vibrant rainbow colors, warm and uplifting atmosphere.
```

## 收势设计说明
- **Clip 1**: 标题字母闪烁 1 次 + 镜头推进 → 中央仙人掌定格（中近景）
- **Clip 2**: 刺特写微动 → 蓝天背景花朵定格
- **Clip 3**: 群像 → 单棵「举手」拟人姿态定格
- **Clip 4**: 高大 → 群像「坚强」定格（情感高潮，停留至最后一帧）
